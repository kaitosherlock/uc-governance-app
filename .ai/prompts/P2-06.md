## 1. Tags tab

On an asset: list its tags, and assign or remove them through `PlanFlow`, never through a bespoke
form. Governed tags come from the tag-policies surface and are constrained — show the allowed values
rather than a free text box, and say where the constraint comes from. A system tag cannot be changed
at all: render it read-only with the reason visible, not merely disabled.

## 2. Policies section

List ABAC policies, and open one to see its scope, tag predicates, included and excluded principals,
and referenced functions. Create, update and delete go through `PlanFlow`.

**The impact panel is the part that must not be got wrong.** The backend returns a `disclaimer`
reading "Potentially affected within your visible scope. Not evaluated by Databricks." and a
`potentially_affected` list, with `meta.limitations` naming what could not be checked. Render the
disclaimer **adjacent to the list itself**, not in a footnote, a tooltip or a collapsed section. A
user glancing at that panel must not be able to come away believing Databricks evaluated it. Do not
title the panel anything like "Effective access" or "Who has access" — it is an approximation and
the wording must say so.

## 3. Filters and masks panel

For a table, show its row filter and column masks with the function, its input columns, their types
and its owner. A control that arrives from an ABAC policy reports `attached_via: "abac_policy"` with
a `policy_id`: render that as a link to the policy and make clear it cannot be changed here. Someone
trying to edit a policy-derived mask at the table otherwise has no idea why their change does not
stick.

Set and drop go through `PlanFlow`. The drop preview already carries what may become visible, what
the backend could not enumerate, and the note that no query was run — render all three. The unknown
list is never collapsed by default.

## 4. Ownership transfer

Available from the asset overview, through `PlanFlow`, with the typed-name confirmation the backend
already requires.

## Applies to all four

## Accessibility

Semantic tables. Every form control has a programmatically associated `<label>` — a placeholder is
not a label. Sortable headers announce sort state. Focus ring visible. Text contrast at least 4.5:1,
UI borders 3:1. Works at 375, 1024 and 1440 px and at 200 percent zoom. `prefers-reduced-motion`
disables transitions. No element carries an ARIA role whose keyboard model it does not implement —
that mistake was already made once with `role="tree"` and removed.
